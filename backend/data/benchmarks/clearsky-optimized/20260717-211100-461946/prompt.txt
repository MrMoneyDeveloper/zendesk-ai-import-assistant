We are setting up Zendesk for a company called ClearSky Insurance Group.
They provide short-term insurance products including vehicle, home contents,
building, and business insurance to individual and corporate clients across
South Africa. They have five support teams: Personal Lines Support,
Commercial Lines Support, Claims, Underwriting, and Client Retention.

Please create the following:

1. Five ticket fields:
   - A dropdown called "Query Type" with options: New Quote Request,
     Policy Amendment, Claim Submission, Claim Status Update,
     Policy Cancellation, Premium Query, Document Request,
     Complaint, General Inquiry
   - A dropdown called "Policy Type" with options: Vehicle Insurance,
     Home Contents, Building Insurance, Business Insurance,
     All Risk Cover, Liability Cover
   - A dropdown called "Client Segment" with options: Individual,
     Small Business, Corporate, High Value Client, Broker Referred
   - A dropdown called "Claim Status" with options: Not Yet Submitted,
     Submitted Awaiting Assessment, Under Investigation,
     Approved Awaiting Payment, Rejected, Closed
   - A text field called "Policy Number"

2. Six triggers:
   - When a ticket is created and Query Type is "Claim Submission"
     and Client Segment is "High Value Client", assign to Claims,
     add tag "hv_claim_submission", and set priority to Urgent
   - When a ticket is created and Query Type is "Claim Submission",
     assign to Claims, add tag "claim_submission", and set priority to High
   - When a ticket is created and Query Type is "Policy Cancellation",
     assign to Client Retention, add tag "cancellation_risk",
     and set priority to High
   - When a ticket is created and Query Type is "Complaint",
     assign to Personal Lines Support, add tag "client_complaint",
     and set priority to High
   - When a ticket is created and Query Type is "New Quote Request"
     and Client Segment is "Corporate", assign to Commercial Lines Support,
     add tag "corporate_quote", and set priority to Normal
   - When a ticket is created and Query Type is "Underwriting"
     and Client Segment is "Broker Referred", assign to Underwriting,
     add tag "broker_referral", and set priority to Normal

3. Five views:
   - All open Claim Submission tickets assigned to Claims
     sorted by priority descending
   - All open Policy Cancellation tickets assigned to Client Retention
     sorted by oldest first
   - All open Complaint tickets assigned to Personal Lines Support
     sorted by oldest first
   - All open Corporate Quote tickets assigned to Commercial Lines Support
     sorted by creation date
   - All unassigned tickets across all teams sorted by oldest first

4. Four macros:
   - A macro called "Acknowledge Claim Submission" that sends a reply:
     "Thank you for submitting your claim with ClearSky Insurance Group.
     Your claim has been received and assigned to our Claims team.
     A claims assessor will contact you within 2 business days to
     confirm receipt and advise on next steps. Please keep all supporting
     documents and evidence available for your assessor."
     and adds tag "claim_acknowledged"
   - A macro called "Request Policy Number" that sends a reply:
     "To locate your policy and assist you accurately, please provide
     your ClearSky policy number which can be found on your policy
     schedule or any previous correspondence from us."
     and adds tag "awaiting_policy_number"
   - A macro called "Retention Offer Outreach" that sends a reply:
     "We are sorry to hear you are considering cancelling your ClearSky
     policy. Before we process your request, we would love the opportunity
     to review your cover and explore options that may better suit your
     current needs and budget. A retention specialist will call you
     within 24 hours." and adds tag "retention_offer_sent"
   - A macro called "Escalate High Value Claim" that assigns the ticket
     to Claims, sets priority to Urgent, adds tag "hv_claim_escalated",
     and adds an internal note: "This claim belongs to a High Value Client
     and requires senior assessor assignment. SLA is 4 hours for first
     contact. Do not process through standard claims queue."

5. Three ticket forms:
   - A form called "ClearSky Personal Lines Form" that includes fields
     in order: Subject, Description, Query Type, Policy Type,
     Client Segment, Policy Number, Priority
   - A form called "ClearSky Claims Form" that includes fields
     in order: Subject, Description, Query Type, Policy Type,
     Client Segment, Claim Status, Policy Number, Priority
   - A form called "ClearSky Commercial Lines Form" that includes
     fields in order: Subject, Description, Query Type, Policy Type,
     Client Segment, Policy Number, Priority

6. Five knowledge base articles:
   - An article called "How to Submit a Claim with ClearSky Insurance"
     in the category Claims that explains the step by step process
     for submitting a vehicle, home, or business insurance claim
     including required documents, timeframes, and what to expect
     after submission
   - An article called "Understanding Your ClearSky Policy Schedule"
     in the category Policy Management that explains what a policy
     schedule contains, how to read your cover limits, excess amounts,
     and how to request amendments
   - An article called "How to Get a Quote for Business Insurance"
     in the category New Business that explains the information
     required for a commercial insurance quote, typical turnaround
     times, and how broker referrals work
   - An article called "What to Do After a Vehicle Accident"
     in the category Claims that provides a step by step emergency
     guide including what information to collect at the scene,
     how to open a claim within 24 hours, and when to contact
     the police
   - An article called "ClearSky Cancellation Policy and Your Options"
     in the category Policy Management that explains the notice
     period required to cancel a policy, potential pro-rata refund
     calculations, and retention options available before cancellation
     is processed
